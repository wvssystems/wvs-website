WVS DMS — secure document management for enterprise workflows

The WVS Document Management System centralizes business-critical files, enriches them with metadata, extracts content with OCR and keeps every version, permission and workflow decision traceable.

  • 3.000+ vendas processadas por mês com fluxos auditáveis
  • 15K+ documentos entre contratos, fotos e anexos analisados automaticamente
  • 200 empresas com dados societários e biométricos sempre atualizados

Built for the path from file to decision

  • Capture documents from portals, sales apps, partners and existing systems.
  • Extract text and metadata with OCR and document intelligence pipelines.
  • Control access, retention, approvals and workflow rules by business context.
  • Track every document status, version and audit event from one dashboard.
